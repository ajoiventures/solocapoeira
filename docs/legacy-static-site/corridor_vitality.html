Methodology: Each district is scored against its own 2015–2019 pre-COVID baseline — not against other districts' raw volumes. Activity Momentum (35%): avg annual permits 2023–25 ÷ avg 2015–19. Values >1.0 = growing vs baseline. Investment Momentum (30%): same ratio for Building Permits only (2022–25 vs 2015–19). Change-of-Use Rate (15%): ChangeUse/ChangeOccu/Conversion as % of all recent permits — space being repurposed. ABL Retention (10%): open/finaled ABL as % of all ABL ever filed — measures liquor-license operating persistence. Risk Rate (10%): stop-work + hard-hold as % of recent permits (inverted; lower = better). 2026 excluded (partial year, data through 2026-05-22).

Validation required: City permit data may contain errors. Scores are investment and planning indicators only — not legal findings. Validate with field survey before reliance on any individual corridor.
